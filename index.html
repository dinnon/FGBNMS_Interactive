Long-Term Reef Cover at the Flower Garden Banks

A CSV-driven viewer for the CPCe benthic monitoring record. Every chart renders directly from the pipeline-product summary files in this folder — reading pre-computed fields like mean_percent_cover and se_percent_cover rather than re-deriving anything from raw point counts.

Photo: FGBNMS / NOAA
If your browser blocks local CSV loading from a double-clicked HTML file, run a local web server from this folder (py -m http.server 8000) or pick the six CSV files above.
FIGURE 01

Long-term benthic cover

Mean percent cover through time at the study sites. East and West Flower Garden Bank are stacked as separate panels. Switch between line trends and a stacked-composition view; turn standard-error bands and provenance shading on or off.

Source: figure3_benthic_time_series_summary.csv + figure3_appendix_benthic_time_series_summary_2004_2008.csv. Overlapping 2004–2008 rows from the two branches are the same measurement and are collapsed to one point per year. Series break at gap years rather than interpolating.
FIGURE 02

Benthic cover composition

Mean percent cover by benthic group for a chosen year. Compare banks and survey scopes side by side, or stack groups to read the full benthic budget. Error bars use the SE field from the CSV.

Source: figure1_random_transects_summary.csv + figure1_appendix_random_transects_summary_2004_2008.csv. Reef-wide scope is only present for recent years.
FIGURE 03

Coral species explorer

Per-species mean cover for a chosen year, ranked by cover. Horizontal bars keep the long taxonomic labels readable; switch to vertical if you prefer. Limit to the top species or show every taxon.

Source: figure2_coral_species_summary.csv + figure2_appendix_coral_species_summary_2004_2008.csv. Ranking uses the summarized CSV cover values only.
FIGURE 04

Coral & macroalgae — phase-shift view

The two CSV cover series plotted directly against each other through time, per bank, with the space between them shaded. Both lines are the mean_percent_cover values straight from the file, grey visualizing changing gap between the lines.

Source: figure3_benthic_time_series_summary.csv + appendix. Only the Coral and Macroalgae mean_percent_cover series are drawn; the shaded band is a visual fill between them, not a derived value.
Provenance. Place this page in the same folder as the six authoritative pipeline product CSVs. It reads summary fields (mean_percent_cover, se_percent_cover, year, bank, survey_scope, and either plot_group or species_label) and a data_source_branch tag. It does not derive percent cover from raw CPCe point data.
